Sec 02 · ADAS Systems

Lane Keeping & Parking Safety

The two ADAS features I work on at Hyundai Mobis — visualized as live system diagrams.

Lane Keeping Assist (LKA)

FEATURE · LANE DEPARTURE PREVENTION

Camera-based lane detection with steering intervention. The system identifies lane boundaries and applies corrective steering when the vehicle drifts, keeping it centered in the lane.

Parking Distance Warning (PDW)

FEATURE · OBSTACLE PROXIMITY ALERT

Ultrasonic sensor-based proximity detection during parking. The system warns the driver of nearby obstacles with distance-based alert zones, escalating from visual to audible warnings as distance decreases.
